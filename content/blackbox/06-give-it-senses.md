We fitted the encoder on a Thursday. Nobody re-zeroed it, so the offset was out by a few degrees — the arm thought it was a little to the left of where it was.

And the ticks-per-turn was set for the old disc. Different resolution, same firmware. So every angle was also stretched by four percent.

Two faults, in one connector, and they cancelled each other near the middle of the range. Near the middle, the arm looked perfect. At the edges it was a different machine.

That is the trap. A fault that hides at the centre of the range will pass every test you run at the centre of the range.
