We were ready. That is the part nobody believes. Two weeks of bring-up, every joint checked, the figure-eight drawing clean on the scope.

The night before the Expo, a patch came down from EA Corp. Helpful. Small. A "startup optimisation" that skipped the long self-check and trusted a cached calibration instead.

We were tired. It was signed off by a reviewer who had four hundred of these that week, and the diff looked like nothing.

It was not nothing. It was four faults in a trench coat, and they all arrived at once, on stage, in front of the people who wanted to prove we could not be trusted.

We could have. We just could not see it in time.
