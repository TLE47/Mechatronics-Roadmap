There is a noise a controller makes when it is about to embarrass you. A rising whine, then the frame starts to chatter, and then you are holding a tuning fork.

Ours did that because the integrator kept charging while the arm was blocked. By the time the arm moved, it had a grudge and eight volts of stored apology.

Two fixes. Stop the integrator when the output is clipped. Then give the controller a term that knows the arm is heavy, so holding still is not a full-time emergency.

After that, the arm lifted a load and held it. Steady. First time in years.

I went outside and stood in the rain for a while.
