I skipped a resistor once. One. Nineteen years old, in a hurry, and the LED was rated for less current than the pin would happily give it.

It lit. Oh, it lit. For about four seconds, and then the magic smoke left and the pin never worked again.

That was the night the arch sign went dark. The collective ate noodles by the light of a phone for a week, and I told them it was a power cut, and they all pretended to believe me.

So now you put the resistor in. Not because the rules say so. Because I am not the only one who has to live with your shortcut.
