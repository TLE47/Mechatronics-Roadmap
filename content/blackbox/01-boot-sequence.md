First board I ever built, I put a green LED on the status pin and called it a night. Beautiful light. Warm. Confident.

The board was reading garbage. The pin went high because it floated, and a floating pin is an optimist.

I shipped eleven of them to a bakery. Every mixer started itself at three in the morning. Nobody was hurt, which is luck, not design.

So: print the number. Not the light. The number. A light is a claim. A printed value is a witness.

The moment you stop asking what it says, you start guessing. Guessing is how this city works now. Not here.
