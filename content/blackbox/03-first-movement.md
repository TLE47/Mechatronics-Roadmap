We tuned H-7 by feel. Hand on the joint, ear on the motor, gut on the gain. It felt good. It sounded good. It was, in a word, wrong.

First power-up, the joint slammed its limit so hard the frame rang. Woke up half the block. Someone upstairs threw a shoe.

Here is what I learned: a joint has a number for how fast it should settle, and a number for how hard it should resist, and neither of those numbers cares how it feels to you at two in the morning.

Measure the response. Then decide what you like. In that order. Every time.
