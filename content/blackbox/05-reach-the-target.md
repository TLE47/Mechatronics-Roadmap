Our arm reached. That was the headline. Two links, a target drawn on the wall, and the tip landing on it.

Except at full extension, the tip landed a hand's width lower every time. And we blamed the target. Moved the target. Re-drew it. Argued about the drawing.

The drawing was fine. The second link was heavier than the model said, and the joint you are not loading is still a joint with a sag.

Reach for the far point first. The near points will flatter you, and flattery is how you ship a machine that is wrong in the only place nobody tested.
