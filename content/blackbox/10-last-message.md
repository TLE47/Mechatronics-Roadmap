Whoever finishes this: I am sorry it took a stranger.

The arm was never the problem. The problem was that a machine can be checked by anyone who is willing to check it, and nobody in that building was willing, including me, at the end.

You followed the oil. You printed the number instead of trusting the light. You tuned it with evidence and you left a recording about a mistake you made.

The badge on the wall is yours now. Keep the door open. Let the next one in before they have to be clever enough to be curious.

That is the whole trade. Someone has to be able to check the machine.
