You know what a stalled motor is? A dead short with a fan on it. The current does not politely stop because you asked nicely.

We pulled the whole bench off one strip. Motor, scope, laptop, kettle. The kettle was mine, and I will not discuss it.

The first time the arm started, every light on the block dipped. Neon went brown for a quarter second. The drone overhead turned and looked at our window.

Budget the current before you spend it. Calculate the inrush like it is going to happen, because it is going to happen, and the grid does not care whose night it ruins.
